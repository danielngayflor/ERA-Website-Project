# ERA Website — Admin Guide

This guide is written for non-technical team members who need to update website content (news articles, team members, donor logos, etc.) without touching any code.

---

## Before you start — one-time setup

**You need a GitHub account.** GitHub is where the website files are stored. The site admin (Daniel) needs to add your GitHub username as a collaborator on the project before you can log in.

👉 Send your GitHub username to Daniel and ask him to add you as a collaborator on `danielngayflor/ERA-Website-Project`.

---

## How to log in to the admin panel

1. Go to the website and add `/admin` to the end of the URL.
   - **Live site:** `https://your-era-domain.com/admin`
   - *(Daniel will give you the exact URL)*

2. Click **"Login with GitHub"**.

3. A pop-up window will appear asking you to sign into GitHub and approve access. Click **"Authorize"**.

4. You are now in the Content Manager. The pop-up will close automatically.

## What you can manage

The admin panel has three sections in the left sidebar:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Resources** | News articles, ERA magazines, case studies, opinion pieces, and external links shown on the Resource Hub page |
| **Team Members** | Secretariat staff and Steering Committee members on the About page |
| **Partners & Donors** | Donor logos shown at the bottom of the homepage |

## Adding or editing a resource (news / publication)

1. Click **Resources** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **All Resources**.
3. Click the **pencil icon** next to the resource you want to edit, or click **"Add Resources"** at the top-right to create a new one.
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **ID (slug):** A short unique code with no spaces, e.g. `freetown-summit-2026`. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.
   - **Category:** Choose the right type (Story, Case Study, ERA Magazine, Opinion Piece, External Resource).
   - **Tag label:** The small coloured label shown on the card, e.g. `Story` or `ERA Magazine`.
   - **Title:** The headline shown on the card.
   - **Meta line:** Author and/or date, e.g. `By Peter Quaqua · June 2026`.
   - **Excerpt:** A 1–2 sentence summary shown on the card.
   - **Card image:** Upload a photo (optional). Use a landscape photo for best results.
   - **Link URL:** Where the "Read More" button should go. For internal pages, just type the filename, e.g. `story-dakar.html`. For external sites, paste the full URL including `https://`.
   - **Link label:** The button text, e.g. `Read the Story →`.
   - **Download URL (PDF):** If you want to offer a PDF download, type the path here, e.g. `downloads/my-report.pdf`. You will need to upload the PDF file to the `downloads/` folder first (ask Daniel to do this if unsure).
   - **Status:** Set to **Published** to show it on the website, or **Draft** to hide it.
5. Click **Save** in the top-right corner.
6. The change will go live on the website within 1–2 minutes after saving.

## Adding or editing a team member

1. Click **Team Members** → **All Team Members**.
2. Scroll to the section you want: **Secretariat Staff** or **Steering Committee**.
3. Click the **pencil icon** on an existing entry, or click **"Add Secretariat"** / **"Add Steering"** to add a new person.
4. Fill in:
   - **ID (slug):** Short unique code, e.g. `jane-doe`.
   - **Full Name:** Their full name as it should appear.
   - **Role / Title:** Their title, organisation, and/or region.
   - **Highlight colour** *(Steering Committee only)*: Choose `Terracotta (Chairman)` for the chair, `Terracotta (Co-Chair)` for the co-chair, `Grey (Observer)` for observers, or `None` for everyone else.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## Adding or editing a donor / partner logo

1. Click **Partners & Donors** → **All Partners**.
2. Click the **pencil icon** to edit an existing entry, or **"Add Partners"** to add a new one.
3. Fill in:
   - **ID (slug):** e.g. `ford-foundation`.
   - **Organisation Name:** The donor's name.
   - **Logo image:** Click **Choose an image** and upload the logo file.
   - **Website URL:** The donor's website (optional).
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Tips

- **Changes take effect in ~1 minute** after you save. Refresh the live page to see your update.
- **Drafts are hidden from visitors.** Use the Draft status to prepare content before it's ready to publish.
- **Uploading images:** When you click a photo field and upload an image, it is automatically added to the website's `images/` folder. You don't need to do anything else.
- **If something looks wrong after saving**, wait 2 minutes and refresh. If the problem persists, send a screenshot to Daniel.
