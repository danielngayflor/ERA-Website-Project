About the Region

Southern Africa's Environmental Rights Context

Southern Africa is a sub-region of striking contrasts — significant natural wealth, relatively more developed legal frameworks in some countries, yet persistent failures to translate law into justice for communities affected by mining, energy extraction, and land dispossession. From Zimbabwe's mining sector to fossil fuel development in Mozambique and Namibia, communities face familiar patterns: displacement without consent, pollution without accountability, and defenders without protection.

ERA's Southern Africa hub, anchored by Oil Change International in Eswatini, connects regional civil society to the continental campaign — building the political will and legal frameworks needed to hold states and corporations accountable. Natural Justice, based in South Africa, also contributes through the Defenders Emergency, Solidarity & Response Working Group, bringing critical capacity in legal advocacy and rapid-response support for defenders at risk.
