What We're Building

Three Pillars of Environmental Democracy

ERA exists to turn environmental rights from a promise into a law — one that guarantees access, participation, and justice for every community on the continent.

Access to Information

Communities have the right to know what's happening to their land, water, and air. Transparency is not a privilege — it's a right.

Public Participation

The people affected by a decision get a seat at the table where it's made. Decisions cannot be imposed on communities without their meaningful involvement.

Access to Justice

When rights are violated, there's a real, enforceable path to remedy. Accountability must go beyond voluntary commitments.

The three pillars of environmental democracy under Principle 10 — currently existing in fragments. ERA is building the framework that makes them binding.

How We Work

Two engines. One movement.

ERA runs on Regional Anchors, who coordinate the movement across Africa's five sub-regions, and Working Groups, who drive the research, advocacy, drafting, and defender-protection work that makes the campaign move.
