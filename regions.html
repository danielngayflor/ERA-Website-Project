Where We Work

One movement. Five regions. A coordinated front across the continent.

ERA's strength comes from its Regional Anchors — organizations designated to coordinate, represent, and advance the coalition's priorities within their sub-region, while staying tightly linked to the Secretariat and the Working Groups.

The Architecture of the Coalition

How the Anchors Connect to the Working Groups

Each Regional Anchor stays tightly linked to the Secretariat and to the six Working Groups — creating a movement that's both locally rooted and continentally coordinated.

The Secretariat

Provides day-to-day coordination, strategic support, and institutional continuity for the entire coalition. Learn more →

Regional Anchors

Translate the coalition's agenda into sub-regional action, represent member organizations, and feed field realities back into the campaign.

Working Groups

Drive the specialized work — legal drafting, case documentation, advocacy, communications, defender protection, and resource mobilization.

See the Working Groups →
