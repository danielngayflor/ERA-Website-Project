Home of the Secretariat

Liberia

ERA's day-to-day coordination is led by the Secretariat, hosted by Green Advocates International, a Liberian-based organization. Liberia isn't just a base of operations — it's also where some of the patterns ERA is fighting have played out directly.

Communities exposed to toxic waste discharge, met with arrests instead of accountability when they spoke up. The same enforcement void ERA is working to close — with a binding regional agreement — made visible in the place where ERA was born.

The Architecture of the Coalition

How the Anchors Connect to the Working Groups

Each Regional Anchor stays tightly linked to the Secretariat and to the six Working Groups — creating a movement that's both locally rooted and continentally coordinated.

The Secretariat

Provides day-to-day coordination, strategic support, and institutional continuity for the entire coalition.

Regional Anchors

Translate the coalition's agenda into sub-regional action, represent member organizations, and feed field realities back into the campaign.

Working Groups

Drive the specialized work — legal drafting, case documentation, advocacy, communications, defender protection, and resource mobilization.
